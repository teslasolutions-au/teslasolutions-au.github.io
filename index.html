What we do

Electrical engineering and installation sit at the centre of our work. Around it, we deliver the infrastructure services projects depend on.

  • Electrical design & installation

    Complete electrical systems for commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects, designed and installed to specification.

    • Design and documentation
    • Wiring and cabling
    • Lighting and small power

  • Power distribution & switchgear

    Reliable distribution from incoming supply to the loads that matter, built for safety and capacity to grow.

    • Switchboards and panels
    • Transformers and supply
    • Backup and standby power

  • Testing, commissioning & maintenance

    We prove that systems perform as designed, then keep them performing with planned and responsive maintenance.

    • Inspection and testing
    • Commissioning
    • Preventive maintenance

  • Infrastructure & civil works

    The groundwork around electrical systems: trenching, ducting, pits and supporting structures.

    • Cable routes and ducts
    • Site services
    • Supporting structures

  • Solar & EV charging

    Connecting new energy to existing sites, from solar arrays to vehicle charging points.

    • Solar integration
    • EV charging installs
    • Load assessment

  • Engineering & project support

    Technical advice and coordination that keeps scope, budget and programme under control.

    • Feasibility and audits
    • Project coordination
    • Compliance support
